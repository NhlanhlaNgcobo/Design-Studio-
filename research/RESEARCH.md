### The real logo

`facebook/logo-original-fb-720.jpg` — their profile picture. Four 3D ink droplets
(cyan, magenta, yellow, black) on curved CMYK ribbons, a large script **EP**
monogram (E cyan, P magenta), *"Eminence Printing"* in an outlined italic script,
and the phone number in yellow. Raster only, 720 px.

It is the most genuinely *theirs* thing found, and the reason the new brand system is
built on process colour rather than on the template's teal. See `../brand/BRAND.md`.

## Local market

No print shop in Empangeni / Richards Bay / uMhlathuze was found with a self-serve
online designer. Nearest comparables are enquiry-only:

- **Emprint** — 3 Brass St, Kuleka, Empangeni — <https://www.emprint.co.za/>
- **Richards Bay Printers** (Alton) — claims 25+ years
- **Custom Print Designs** — Empangeni CBD
- **3@1** — Boardwalk Inkwazi, Richards Bay

National online sellers run local landing pages (*offprint.co.za/t-shirt-printing-empangeni*,
*tshirtsdurban.com/t-shirts-empangeni*). They are the real competition for the
small-run T-shirt buyer, which is exactly who the Studio is for.

Unrelated businesses with the same name, ignored: Eminence Print Co. (Stockton, CA),
Eminence Group (Kempton Park), Eminence Books.
