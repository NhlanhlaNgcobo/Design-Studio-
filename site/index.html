What artwork to send

The single biggest cause of a disappointing print is a small file stretched big. This is what each kind of job needs.

Printing onBestIf it's a photo or PNG
Clothing, caps, mugsVector — AI, PDF, SVG or EPS300 dpi at print size — about 3,300 px wide for a 280 mm chest print
Business cards, flyersPDF with 3 mm bleed, text 4 mm inside the edge300 dpi
PostersPDF150 dpi at full size
Pull-up bannersPDF at full size120 dpi at full size
Banners, gazebos, signs, vehiclesVector72–100 dpi at full size
