# Provenance of every studio blank

One line per file: file - how it was made - date.

- `tee-front.png` - S.i. Graphics *FREE T-Shirt Mockup Template (FRONT).psd* (bought by the owner): the template's silhouette mask, "Darks" and "Lights" layers over light grey, rebuilt by `build/blanks/psd_blanks.py` - 2026-11-05
- `tee-back.png` - the same front, mirrored, neck scoop filled with fabric and a back neck band drawn (the free pack has no back view) - 2026-11-05
- `hoodie-front.png` - S.i. Graphics *FREE Hoodie Mockup Template (FRONT).psd*: the photographed black hoodie's luminance normalised to shading, lightly smoothed - 2026-11-05
- `oversized-front.png`, `oversized-back.png` - the tee blanks reshaped boxy (20 % wider, 6 % shorter) by `psd_blanks.py`, so they share the tee's photograph - 2026-11-05
- `polo-front.png`, `polo-back.png` - the tee blanks with a collar, stand, placket and buttons built by `psd_blanks.py` from the tee's own fabric and shading (no golf-shirt photo exists in the packs) - 2026-11-05

The pack's licence PDF says *"Personal Use Only"*. Only these derived blanks are
published, never the PSDs. Before launch, get S.i. Graphics (info@sigraphics.co)
to confirm commercial print-on-demand use, or buy their commercial / full
(front + back) version, which would also give a real photographed back.
