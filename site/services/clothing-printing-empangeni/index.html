Clothing printing and embroidery in Empangeni

Golf shirts, T-shirts, hoodies and caps for staff, schools, clubs and events — screen printed for big runs, embroidered for the corporate look.

Screen printEmbroiderySublimationDTF

What we print on

  • Golf shirts — the staple of corporate and school wear. Branded on the chest, sleeve or back; the placket rules out a full-front print.
  • T-shirts, regular and oversized, for events, campaigns, clubs and merch.
  • Hoodies — front print sits above the kangaroo pocket; the back takes the big one.
  • Caps — embroidered on the front panel, like our Ant Ranch job.
  • Tote bags for events and shops.

Which method

  • Screen printing gives the best price on bigger runs of the same design. It is priced per ink colour, so a one- or two-colour logo is the sweet spot.
  • Embroidery is stitched thread: hard-wearing and smart, best for chest logos and caps. We send a digitised proof before stitching.
  • Sublimation dyes the print into white or light polyester — it never cracks or peels. It cannot go on dark or cotton garments.
  • DTF transfers print full colour on any fabric colour, and suit small quantities and photos.

What to send

  • Your logo as a vector (AI, PDF, SVG or EPS) if you have it. Otherwise a PNG at least 300 dpi at the printed size — around 3,300 pixels wide for a 280 mm chest print.
  • Sizes and quantities per size, and the garment colour.
  • Not sure? Put your logo on a shirt in the Design Studio — it checks the resolution and counts the ink colours for you.
