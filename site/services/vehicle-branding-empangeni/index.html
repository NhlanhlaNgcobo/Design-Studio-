Vehicle branding and fleet livery in Empangeni

Fleet livery, door branding and part or full wraps — printed, laminated and fitted by our team. We branded the Khula Med ambulance fleet.

Printed vinylCut vinylLaminationFitting

What we do

  • Fleet livery for panel vans and minibuses, like the Khula Med ambulances on Toyota Quantums.
  • Door and side branding for bakkies and cars.
  • Cut-vinyl lettering — the budget option for names, numbers and contact details.
  • Printed and laminated graphics for full-colour sides.

How it is quoted

  • Every vehicle is quoted after we see it: panel condition, curves and how much of the body is covered all change the fitting time.
  • Use the Design Studio to put your branding on a Quantum-class van and send us the picture — it gives an indicative range, and we confirm the price after seeing the vehicle.

What to send

  • Your logo as a vector file (AI, PDF, SVG or EPS).
  • The make, model and year of each vehicle, and a photo of each side.
  • What has to appear: name, phone, website, any registration or regulatory markings.
